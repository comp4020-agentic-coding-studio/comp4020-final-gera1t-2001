# Crit 9 reflection

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

The breakthrough was an idea. Last week I had only an account system and no
idea what the website was for. This week I decided to make a Texas Hold'em
site for friends, built on top of the open-source rules engine I chose and the
login system I already had. Once I had that idea, the account system stopped
being a placeholder and became the base: every player at the table is an
account, and the work had a direction.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

Two things this week.

First, I should double-check my own work before I hand it to the agent. I
changed the brief and told the agent to carry on, but the new version had
never actually reached the repo. The agent noticed and stopped to ask me. That
told me I had not finished my part, and next time I should check it myself.

Second, all tests passing is not enough. The checks were green, but a real
browser still found two bugs: a form that should have been hidden was still on
screen, and the auto-fold label named the wrong player. I want to be a
developer who looks at the real thing in a real browser, not one who stops at
a green tick.
